Pulse Play

Terms

These terms cover the Pulse Play overlay site at https://chriskeys19-bot.github.io/pulseplay-cricketstream-overlays/.

YouTube controls are for Pulse Play admins. Those controls start and end live broadcasts on the CricketCrazeUSA YouTube channel only.

Connecting YouTube lets Pulse Play read that channel's live broadcast list and change a broadcast's status. Use Start and End only for CricketCrazeUSA's own broadcasts.

Pulse Play does not sell personal data. The token that keeps the YouTube connection is stored on the server. An admin, or the owner of the Google account, can revoke that access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The YouTube channel remains subject to YouTube's own terms. This site does not replace them.
