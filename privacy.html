Privacy policy

This policy covers the Pulse Play overlay site at https://chriskeys19-bot.github.io/pulseplay-cricketstream-overlays/.

Pulse Play uses Google sign-in only so its admins can start and end live broadcasts on the CricketCrazeUSA YouTube channel. That channel uses the Google account cricketcrazeusa@gmail.com.

With that permission, Pulse Play reads the channel's live broadcast list and changes a broadcast's status. It does not upload videos, post comments, or change any other Google data.

The YouTube refresh token is stored on the server. It is not stored in the browser, and it is not shown on the admin page. A signed-in admin can see whether YouTube is connected and which channel name came back.

Pulse Play does not sell personal data. It does not share the YouTube token or the broadcast list with advertisers or data brokers. YouTube receives a status change only when an admin starts or ends a broadcast.

Signing in to the overlay admin is separate from the YouTube connection. Overlay pages that a YoloBox loads do not sign in and do not use this permission.

You can revoke this Google access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After it is revoked, Pulse Play cannot read or change broadcasts until an admin connects YouTube again.

Questions: cricketcrazeusa@gmail.com.
